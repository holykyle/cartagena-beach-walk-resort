About Cartagena Beachwalk Resort

Located in Guiwang, Alcoy, Cebu, this coastal gem offers a tranquil escape along the famous white sands of Southern Cebu. Situated just steps away from Tingko Beach, it provides the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation.

The Experience

Known for its laid-back atmosphere and stunning sunrise views over the Cebu Strait.

Activities

Enjoy beach volleyball, snorkeling in nearby coral patches, or a dip in the calm, shallow waters.

Location

Easily accessible along the Natalio Bacalso Highway, close to Tinko Beach and Voda Krasna.
